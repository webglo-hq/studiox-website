# Tie-out playbook

| Symptom | Likely cause | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Ledger AP lower than open bills | Payment recorded twice, or payment without a bill | Reclass the excess to vendor refunds receivable; request refund |
| Ledger AP higher than open bills | Manual adjustment to the control account, accrual booked to AP | Reverse or move to accrued liabilities; get the vendor statement |
| Ledger AR higher than open invoices | Revenue or AR adjustment without an invoice | Obtain support; reverse if unsupported (occurrence) |
| Ledger AR lower than open invoices | Cash applied in the ledger but not the subledger | Apply the receipt in the subledger |

Control accounts should only be changed through their subledger. Manual entries to a control
account are an audit red flag (PCAOB AS 2401) even when the amount is small.

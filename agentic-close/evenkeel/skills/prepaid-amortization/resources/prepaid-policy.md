# Prepaid policy (Kitewell, fictional)

- Record as prepaid any payment of **$5,000 or more** covering more than one month of future
  service (software subscriptions, insurance, maintenance) — ASC 340-10.
- Amortise straight-line by month over the service period starting in the first service
  month; the last month absorbs rounding so the schedule totals exactly.
- Prepaid software → 1210, prepaid insurance → 1220, other → 1200.
- A missed month in a closed period is caught up in the current period and reported to the
  controller as a control exception; closed periods are never reopened for amortisation.

# Close package template

1. Executive commentary (draft by agent, approved by controller)
2. Consolidated balance sheet (vs prior month)
3. Consolidated income statement — month (vs prior month)
4. Entity statements (Kitewell US in USD, Kitewell UK in GBP)
5. Reconciliation status (balances, differences, preparer, reviewer)
6. Close checklist (tasks, owners, completion)
7. Variance commentary (approved explanations)
8. Exceptions (open and resolved)
9. Adjustments booked during the close (who prepared, who approved or which policy)
10. FX rates used; agent activity (runs, proposals, automation rate, cost)

The package is hashed (SHA-256 over the signed sections). Approval refuses a package whose
numbers changed after it was generated.

# Fixed asset policy (Kitewell, fictional)

| Class | Account | Useful life |
|---|---|---|
| Computer equipment | 1500 | 36 months |
| Furniture and fixtures | 1510 | 84 months |
| Leasehold improvements | 1510 | shorter of lease term or 120 months |

- Capitalise at **$2,500** or more per purchase (ASC 360).
- Straight-line, no salvage, **full-month convention**: depreciation starts in the month the
  asset is placed in service. The final month absorbs rounding.
- Depreciation expense follows the department of the asset.

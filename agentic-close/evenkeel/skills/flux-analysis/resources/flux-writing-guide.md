# Writing variance explanations

1. **Number first.** "Marketing & Advertising rose $112,340 (+226%) vs August."
2. **Drivers with amounts.** "Brightstage Summit sponsorship ($95,000, one-time); Signal Ads
   August campaign true-up ($18,450)."
3. **Recurring or one-time.** Say which, so FP&A can update the forecast.
4. **Coverage.** "These explain 98% of the change." If below 80%, say what is left.
5. **No jargon.** A CFO reads it on a phone in ten seconds.

Thresholds (Kitewell): a line is material at **$25,000 and 10%** of the comparison.
Explanations are approved by the controller; the preparer cannot approve their own.

# Intercompany policy (Kitewell, fictional)

- Kitewell Labs, Inc. (US, USD) charges Kitewell Labs Ltd (UK, GBP) a monthly management fee
  of **USD 25,000**; shared costs are recharged at cost with reference `IC-YYYY-MM-XX`.
- Intercompany balances are denominated in **USD**. The UK records them in GBP at the average
  rate and remeasures them at the closing rate each month (see fx-revaluation).
- Both sides must record every transaction in the same month with the same reference.
- Settlement is quarterly by wire from the UK to the US.
- Consolidation (ASC 810): eliminate intercompany revenue/expense for the month and the
  due-from / due-to balances at month end in the consolidation entity. Balance eliminations
  are reversing entries.

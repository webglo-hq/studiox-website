# FX policy (Kitewell, fictional)

- **Remeasurement** (ASC 830-20): monetary items denominated in a foreign currency are
  remeasured into the functional currency at the closing rate; the difference goes to
  **7000 FX Gain/Loss** in profit or loss.
- **Translation** (ASC 830-30): the UK's GBP statements are translated into USD for
  consolidation — balance sheet at closing, income statement at monthly average, equity at
  historical rates; the difference is the cumulative translation adjustment in equity.
- Rates: month-end closing and monthly average rates from the treasury rate feed (fictional
  rates in this demo).

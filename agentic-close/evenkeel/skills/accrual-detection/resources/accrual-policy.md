# Accrual policy (Kitewell, fictional)

- Accrue every unrecorded liability above **$500** per vendor per month.
- Estimates: exact amount when a bill exists; PO value pro-rated by service days for goods
  received not invoiced; median of the last three months for recurring vendors billed in arrears.
- Payroll: earned wages and employer taxes from the last pay-period end to month end, by
  department, at the latest run's daily rate.
- All accruals are **auto-reversing** on the first day of the next month; the vendor bill
  then posts normally and only the estimate difference remains.
- Accruals go to 2100 Accrued Liabilities (2110 for payroll), never to Accounts Payable.

# Schedules

- Depreciation lines are generated when an asset is placed in service: cost ÷ useful life per
  month, remainder on the last line, so the lines sum exactly to cost (AX-SCH-01).
- Accumulated depreciation can never exceed cost minus salvage (AX-SCH-02).
- Prepaid schedules spread an annual payment evenly over its term; the remainder sits on the last
  month.
- A scheduled line with no journal entry after its month has passed is a **missed** posting: post
  it in the current open month as a catch-up, never in a closed month.

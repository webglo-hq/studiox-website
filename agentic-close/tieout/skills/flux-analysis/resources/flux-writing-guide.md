# Writing variance explanations

Good:
> Events and conferences rose $102,141 (+1,204%) from August to $110,625. The driver is ConfabCon
> Events, new this month (+$110,000), partly offset by no Atrium workshop (−$7,751). Against the
> $11,958 budget the month is $98,667 over.

Weak:
> Events were higher due to more events.

Checklist:
1. Number first, with direction, amount and percentage.
2. Drivers with names and amounts; they must add up to the variance.
3. Separate timing (accruals, reversals, catch-ups) from real change.
4. Name pending items by proposal reference and say what the month would be once approved.
5. Compare to budget in one clause.

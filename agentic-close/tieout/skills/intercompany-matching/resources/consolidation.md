# Consolidation notes (ASC 810 and ASC 830)

- Intercompany balances are compared in the **transaction currency**; a reporting-currency
  difference within the FX tolerance is rounding.
- The missing side of a one-sided entry is booked by the entity that omitted it, at the rate on the
  date it is recorded (period end, so the closing rate).
- Translation: assets and liabilities at the closing rate, income and expenses at the monthly
  average rate, equity at historical rates; the plug is the cumulative translation adjustment.
- Eliminations live in the elimination entity (BWL-ELIM) so legal-entity books stay clean.
  Revenue and expense eliminations stay for the month; balance eliminations reverse next month.
- Rate differences between the two sides of an elimination go to 7120 Intercompany FX difference.
- Eliminations are computed pro forma when true-ups or remeasurements are still pending, and can
  only be approved after them.

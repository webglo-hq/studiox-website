# Capitalization policy (Bramblewood Labs, fictional)

- Threshold: **$5,000** per item (policy-tunable: `capitalizationMinor`).
- Useful lives: computer equipment 36 months, lab equipment 60 months, furniture 84 months,
  leasehold improvements the shorter of the lease term or 120 months.
- Depreciation: straight-line, no salvage, starting the month **after** the asset is placed in
  service.
- Software subscriptions (SaaS) are expensed monthly; annual prepayments go to 1220 Prepaid
  software and amortize over the term.
- Signals of a capital item: equipment keywords (spectrometer, analyzer, workstation, server,
  furniture), equipment MCCs (5045, 5047, 5732, 5021), and a vendor whose default account is PP&E.

The reclass entry for an expensed capital item debits the PP&E account and credits the expense
account, same department; the fixed-asset record and its schedule are created on approval.

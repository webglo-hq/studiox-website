# ASC 830 remeasurement

Monetary items (cash, receivables, payables, intercompany balances) denominated in a currency other
than the entity's functional currency are remeasured at each balance-sheet date at the closing rate.
The change in the functional-currency amount is an unrealized transaction gain or loss in profit or
loss (7110).

Worked example: a GBP payable of £120,000 booked at 1.3092 is carried at $157,104. At a closing
rate of 1.3412 it is $160,944, so the liability grew by $3,840: an unrealized loss (debit 7110,
credit the payable).

Non-monetary items (inventory, prepaids, PP&E) stay at historical rates.

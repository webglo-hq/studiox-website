# Subledger tie-out procedure

1. Rebuild the aging from documents: bills (or invoices) posted on or before the period end, less
   payments (or receipts) applied on or before the period end.
2. Compare the total with the GL control account balance at the period end.
3. Common causes of differences: entries posted straight to the control account, documents posted
   after the period end with an earlier GL date, voided payments still applied, FX on foreign
   documents.
4. Wrong-sign balances: a vendor with a debit balance is a receivable (overpayment, duplicate
   payment, credit note); a customer with a credit balance is a liability (unapplied cash, credit
   note). Reclassify material ones for presentation.

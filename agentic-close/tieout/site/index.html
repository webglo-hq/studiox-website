Ten close skills, one checklist

The work your team does every month, prepared before they arrive.

Bank reconciliation

Matches statements to the ledger (exact, split settlements, deposit batches), explains deposits in transit and outstanding checks, drafts fee and interest entries.

Transaction coding

Codes card spend with your mappings, plain-English rules and history, flags misclassified posts and purchases that should be capitalized.

Accruals

Finds bills that arrived after month-end, vendors that usually bill but didn’t, goods received not invoiced, and payroll earned but unpaid.

Schedules & tie-outs

Rolls depreciation and prepaid schedules and ties the AP and AR subledgers to their control accounts.

Intercompany & FX

Agrees both sides of every intercompany balance, remeasures foreign-currency items and drafts eliminations for the group view.

Review & reporting

Scores manual entries for risk, drafts variance explanations whose drivers add up exactly, and assembles the close package.

Runs in dependency order, across entities

The close checklist is a graph: coding before accruals, FX before eliminations. Tieout runs each wave of agents per entity, records every step, resumes after a crash without duplicating work, and stops at its budget.

Built for controllers and auditors

Controls you can prove, not promise.

  • Autonomy per skill, set by the Controller and capped by the skill’s manifest.
  • Maker-checker and “whoever ran the agent cannot approve it”, enforced by the app and by database triggers.
  • Approval limits route large items to the right person.
  • A hash-chained audit trail of every change, by people and agents, verifiable on demand.
  • Third-party text is treated as data: instruction-like memos are withheld and held for a person.
  • Tenant isolation enforced by PostgreSQL row-level security.

Grounded, not guessed

An accounting ontology the agents can’t argue with.

Every account, entry, reconciliation and control is a class in Tieout’s close ontology, with executable rules: entries balance, posted entries never change, drivers add up to the variance. Agents are grounded in it, every proposal is checked against it, and every underlined term in the app explains itself.

Open by design

Fits the stack you already run.

Bank and card feeds

BAI2, ISO 20022 camt.053, OFX and CSV statements; card, ERP journal, AP and payroll imports. Idempotent, with provenance to the file and line.

Claude, live or offline

Skills run on Claude with typed tools, or on a deterministic offline engine with the same verifier, routing and audit. Golden evals gate every change.

MCP and open data

An MCP server gives Claude read access and agent runs with scoped tokens. The ontology exports as OWL in JSON-LD and Turtle.
